### <center>pacific void centre?</center>
a few days back i thought about giving my website a proper name. during the time i also came up with my own imaginary music project (in the sense of *Ginger Root*, where it's not really defined as a musical band). then i named it *Pacific Void Centre* (also call it *Void Centre*). despite coming up with it, i don't yet plan to make and release music under the name yet. so i simply transferred the name back to this website. i will probably reserve *Void Centre* for other purposes.
