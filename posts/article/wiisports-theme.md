# rambler
alright. the wii just turned 20... a few days ago. yay. im sure im pretty late to the anniversary now, as the wii was released on 19 november 2006. i dont know if nintendo is doing any festive events in memory of the tiny set-top-box like device. it seems like the attentions are now all centred around a video game released roughly 20 years after, that everyone and-their-mums know what it is. well. that doesnt matter. i dont care about it that much myself. 

you know. i had never really been an avid nintendo fan. so its a bit unusual for me to talk about it. so just a little heads up. i know a lot of people around the world had their childhood or memories revolved around this little piece of technology. i... didnt though, but am still at least see the wii quite an interesting device. only if it had HD support and ""*slightly*"" better specifications, though. it would be so much better.

its done for the wii now. however, there is something that has been stuck in my mind for so long, across time and space, remaining around yet makes me comfortable and calm every time i think about it. yep. thats the *Wii Sports title theme*.

# what

yes. im not sure if youre asking that yourself. just thought its funny to leave that as a header.

anyway, a bit of the background. wii sports was released the same day as the wii, and was included with the console in almost anywhere in the world except japan and korea, for some reason. for most people, its a way to connect and do exercise, and certainly have fun too. but for me, its perhaps a different experience.

# my memories

i have played wii sports before though, just so little. i played some bowling if i remember correctly. but the whole thing was just by how much i appreciated its title theme song. i actually paused at the main menu once to appreciate the masterpiece kazumi totaka brought to us as a video game soundtrack.

it was genuinely upbeat, cheerful and has some kind of a warming-up feeling. imagine a television sign-on run or a morning continuity section in the 2000s. i cannot give an example of actual television network or channel using a similar song. but at least from my vague memory, i think it was used in a continuity/transitional section in a local tv channel unlicensed. given where i live back then (spoiler: vietnam), nintendo wouldnt have done anything to stop that.

when i was younger (from my toddling all the way to elementary school), the primary media format i was exposed to was television. there were only really little internet mentions though. so thats where im pretty confident at. but false memory happens, so dont mind it too much. 

at some point, when i think about the title theme, i imagine sitting on a large, open pasture looking at a forest nearby in a cloudy, whitey sky. yeah its not even blue. and feeling the breeze running around all over the imagined location. honestly theres not much to say about this or i dont know if you can imagine it the same way i do. but if you have time right now, go give it a listening. im sure its really pleasing.

# something that still doesnt click

now i know what it really is, its good to let go of the inner longing, should we? no. 

earlier this year i realized the wii would turn 20 later. i immediately thought about the wii sports title theme. and was curious about when exactly it, along with the rest of the wii sports soundtracks, was composed. this is a difficult matter since nintendo didnt really give much information for their video game soundtracks. they were never their own songs released, but just supplied as a video game bgm. 

if you think it is silly to do that. yes i admit, it must be such a weird thing to scrutinize about. but simply its just my ardent feeling about looking for any clues when it first happened. and that redirected my searching effort to the E3 2006 event, where the wii was introduced for the first time to the world, with working demonstrated models and tech demos of some nintendo games. 

at first i searched thru a nintendo E3 video i found on the internet, that was roughly an hour long, to no avail. then follow videos recorded by individuals attending the event. it actually took me about 20 minutes during bedtime when i was still staying up just looking thru every video i found, winding them back and forth, and trying to identify parts with wii sports gameplay. there were indeed wii sports gameplay videos, but seemingly the title theme was missing. the search briefly ended as i was tired and was to get to sleep. all to satisfy my curiosity that whether the wii sports title theme was a thing back in E3 2006, or was there an early version of the theme that was since lost in time.

but not long after that. at the wee hours of 19 november i attempted to find again. and it seems like there was a video of the title theme, supposedly as it was played in E3.

# bottom lines

after all, the wii sports title theme, as i can say, is truly what makes us recognize the game. its a prominent identity marker, but more than that, its also used in various other kind of media, like memes, essay videos (esp. about the wii sports), and perhaps many more. 

for me, it is truly a beautiful piece of music, but at the same time, i cannot regard it the way any other kinds of instrumental music can be done so. it has no title of its own, only dubbed "title theme" and wii sports was its only cause of existence. but knowing that doesnt change the way i appreciate it, by the tranquil feeling it has brought to me over the time, and how it briefly appeared in my childhood, though just faintly, which was probably the reason for all of this.
